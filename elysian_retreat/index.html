A Luxurious Gated Estate in Idukki, Kerala

Perched high on a hilltop where the fresh mountain air meets lush tropical greenery, Elysian Heights offers an enchanting escape into the heart of Kerala.

Designed with a harmonious blend of modern aesthetics and natural grandeur, this 4 BHK villa overlooks a stunning vista of the Periyar River. Enjoy absolute peace of mind inside a fully secure property equipped with an automated gate and CCTV cameras. With two on-site caretakers and a professional cook available on request, your stay is guaranteed to be effortless and unforgettable.
