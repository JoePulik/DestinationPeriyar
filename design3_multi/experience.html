The Experience

Gaze out over misty valleys and discover the premium outdoor features of the estate

Panoramic Sitout Area

Our main sitout patio is positioned at the edge of the hilltop, offering an unobstructed, birds-eye view of the river winding through the rocks and trees. Watch the sunrise paint the fog or watch the river reflect the moonlight in peaceful silence.

Open Sunset Bar

Designed for social evenings, the terrace features an open-air bar counter where you can mix drinks while taking in a 360-degree view of the Idukki mountain range. It is the perfect place to watch the sunset with family and friends.

Landscaped Gardens

The estate is enveloped by beautifully manicured hilltop gardens, featuring local flowering plants, stone pathways, and grass terraces. Stroll through the lawns or sit on the benches to soak up the mountain environment.

4 Large Balconies

The upper level of the villas includes four separate, expansive balconies. Offering private viewpoints for different rooms, they ensure every guest has a front-row seat to the beautiful natural landscape.

Gated Security & CCTV

Your privacy and safety are paramount. The estate is secured by a modern automated main gate that restricts unauthorized entry. The entire perimeter is continuously monitored by a high-definition CCTV camera system.

Caretakers & Cook Service

Two dedicated caretakers reside on the property to assist with check-in, cleaning, and basic guest services. Additionally, a professional chef can be arranged on request to cook authentic Kerala meals or customized cuisines.
