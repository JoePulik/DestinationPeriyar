Villa Details & Floor Layouts

A structured breakdown of the architectural spaces and room specifications at the estate.

ACCOMMODATION

4 BHK Luxury Suites

The estate consists of four independent bedroom suites. Each bedroom features a spacious layout with attached, fully-tiled bathrooms, modern sanitary fittings, and ventilation systems. They are designed to operate either as a collective 4 BHK family rental or as individual private suites with communal living spaces.

  • Attached private bathrooms in all 4 rooms
  • Large wardrobes and vanity areas
  • Overlooking the valley or landscaped gardens
LEISURE SPACES

3 Lounges & Open Sunset Bar

Designed for entertainment and group activities, the property features three separate drawing rooms. Each drawing room is equipped with oversized comfortable sofas. On the upper terrace, the open bar counter allows for hosting cocktail parties and social gatherings with views of the sunset.

  • Three drawing rooms with custom luxury sofas
  • Dedicated open-air bar counter on the terrace
  • 4 large balconies providing private outdoor spaces
UTILITIES & BUSINESS

Dual Kitchens, Dining & Office Room

The estate is equipped with two complete kitchens, featuring modern cooking appliances and double-cylinder gas connections, providing independent food preparation capabilities for guests. The property also features a formal dining area, a common guest bathroom, and a quiet, dedicated office room suitable for managers or work-from-home needs.

  • 2 fully-equipped kitchens with appliances
  • 1 formal dining area & 1 common guest bathroom
  • 1 dedicated office room for work/management
