Rooms & Spaces

A comprehensive tour of the interiors and accommodations at our hilltop estate

01 / SLEEPING SUITES

4 Bedrooms with Attached Bathrooms

Each of the four bedrooms is designed as an independent sanctuary of rest, providing custom luxury bedding, wardrobes, and ventilation optimized for the cooling mountain breeze. Every bedroom features its own private attached bathroom with premium bath fittings, running hot water, and complimentary luxury toiletries.

Ideal for families traveling together, corporate groups, or individual travelers booking separate rooms who still value their own personal space and total privacy.

02 / SOCIAL SPACES

3 Large Drawing Rooms

The villa boasts three distinct living and drawing rooms. Each space is carefully furnished with oversized, ultra-comfortable custom sofas, providing plenty of room for all guests to relax, read, or socialize.

One lounge is set up on the upper floor, giving access to the massive balconies, while the main drawing room on the ground floor serves as the perfect reception area for guests.

03 / CULINARY HUB

2 Fully Equipped Kitchens

We provide two full-size kitchens inside the property. They are outfitted with high-end appliances, refrigerators, microwaves, and double-burner gas connections, giving you the flexibility to cook your own meals if desired.

The kitchens are stocked with all basic utensils, dinnerware, and cookware, making long-term stays highly convenient.

🍽️ Dining Area

A separate, beautifully appointed formal dining room adjacent to the kitchen, perfect for family dinners or collaborative lunches.

💼 Private Office Room

A quiet office workspace containing a desk and chair, coupled with estate-wide high-speed WIFI. Perfect for remote work or handling business operations during your stay.

🚻 Common Bathroom

A convenient additional common bathroom located near the main drawing room and dining area, ideal for visiting guests.
