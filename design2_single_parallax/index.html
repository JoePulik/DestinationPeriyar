01 / CONCEPT

A Bold Modern Statement in the Highlands

Vanya Riverfront stands as a bold statement of modern architecture nestled in the untouched hills of Idukki, Kerala.

With a design that balances clean geometric lines, striking terracotta accents, and expansive glass facades, this hilltop villa is oriented to maximize privacy and capture the spectacular valley views. Facing the Periyar River, the twin villas are oriented to capture the sun's travel, culminating in spectacular nocturnal illuminations.

It is equipped as a high-security gated property, featuring automated entry and full CCTV cameras, ensuring guests can enjoy their open bar, landscaped gardens, and hilltop sitout in absolute peace and tranquility.
